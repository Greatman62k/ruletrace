# UD-23 Reviewer identity check

Status: **Confirmed**

Decision: the user accepted the recommendation. The Review_Group compares the Reviewer name by exact equality (Unicode scalar values, no normalization, no case folding, no trimming) with the identities recorded in the Origin metadata of the Rule_Revision and in the Proposal_Store batch. Residual risk R-8 (names are not authenticated) is documented. The hardening option below is not adopted in the first version.

Affects: Requirements 23.15, 26.17. Design §3.8.3 (step 4, `plan_review`), §9.2, and the `Reviewer` type of design §3.8 (`Reviewer::new`, `Reviewer::check_not_ai`). Consumer: `rt-review` (task 6.1).

## Options

| Option | Comparison set | Cost | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recommended: exact comparison with Origin and batch identities | The proposer identity, the AI_Provider identifier, the model identifier, and any AI identity recorded in the Origin metadata of the target Rule_Revision or in its Proposal_Store entry (batch) | Small, local to the target revision. No store-wide scan | **Chosen** |
| Hardening: compare with all identities in the store | Every AI identity recorded anywhere in the Proposal_Store and in all Origin metadata of the Rulebook, not only those of the target | A full scan per review, and a Reviewer may be refused because of an unrelated batch. Still no authentication | Not adopted; may be revisited after the first version |

## Rules applied (Req 23.15, 26.17)

- Empty Reviewer, or longer than 200 Unicode scalar values: `REVIEW_REVIEWER_INVALID` (`RT-APR-0202`), Severity Error, exit 3.
- Reviewer equal to a proposer, provider, model or recorded AI identity of the target: `REVIEW_SELF_APPROVAL` (`RT-APR-0201`), Severity Error, exit 3.
- On refusal the Rule_Revision, Approval_Ledger, Proposal_Store and Rulebook stay byte-identical and no Ledger_Record is appended.
- The Reviewer identity comes only from a command argument. The `Reviewer` type has no AI variant.

## Residual risk R-8

The check compares strings. It does not authenticate a person. Whoever can write the ledger can append any name, and a person can choose a name that differs from every recorded AI identity (for example a spelling variant, because no normalization or case folding is applied). The hash chain detects edits of earlier records but not a complete rewrite by a person with write access. R-8 is documented in the user guide (Req 26.22). Authentication of Reviewer identity is deferred beyond the first version (requirements, deferred list).
