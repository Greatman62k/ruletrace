# UD-39: RuleTrace Laboratory final design decisions

Confirmed by the user on 2026-11-18 after the design and threat-model report. This record states what was decided, what stays open and the consequences. The user's instructions are summarised faithfully; quoted phrases are the user's.

## A. Credential architecture (resolves LT-1, LT-2; supersedes the open part of LT-3)

For the current submission:

1. The Laboratory uses the offline demonstration workflow.
2. Live provider requests are initiated by the user, who runs `ruletrace-ai` in their own terminal with the existing credential mechanism.
3. The generated proposal is saved as a hand-off artifact.
4. The user imports that artifact into the Laboratory.
5. The Laboratory validates the artifact, displays its origin and content hash, and treats it as untrusted until explicit human approval.

No OS credential-store integration in this release. Requirement 28 remains unchanged. Requirement 36.2 and the design and traceability text say honestly that live AI generation is external in this version, and that proposal import and all later stages are integrated. The offline demonstration identifies its responses as authored scripts, not provider output.

Environment isolation:

- The Laboratory receives no provider credential variable.
- Supported launch scripts for the supported platforms build a clean, allow-listed environment for `ruletrace-lab`.
- One registry of provider credential environment-variable **names** (never values).
- Tests verify that every registered credential variable is excluded from the Laboratory launch environment.
- The Laboratory does not read, retain, display, transmit or log a credential value.
- The existing credential mechanism of `ruletrace-ai` is not altered.
- The Laboratory starts `ruletrace` with a clean, explicit environment as well, and never forwards arbitrary inherited variables to a subprocess.
- No claim of OS-level isolation against another process of the same user. The actual boundary is stated.

## B. LT-4 to LT-9

| Item | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| LT-4 | Approved: standard-library HTTP server and server-rendered forms. Loopback binding, Host and Origin validation, CSP, filesystem and request-validation restrictions retained. |
| LT-5 | Approved: restart after a session ends. A Launch_Secret is never reused across sessions. |
| LT-6 | **Deferral not accepted. OPEN.** Investigate an origin-scoped authentication mechanism or another demonstrably safe solution. Cookies are not port-isolated; SameSite and Host validation do not by themselves remove cross-port cookie exposure. Open until the threat model and an independent reviewer accept the selected mitigation. |
| LT-7 | **Uncontrolled subprocesses outliving a crashed Laboratory rejected. OPEN until demonstrated.** Design process containment for all supported platforms; children are terminated and reaped when the Laboratory exits or crashes; crash tests show no orphan remains. If this cannot be demonstrated on a supported platform, report the limitation and revise the architecture rather than claim protection. |
| LT-8 | Approved: 16 MiB per uploaded file, 64 MiB aggregate per session, 1 MiB per ordinary control request. Limits enforced while streaming, before unbounded buffering or disk writes; partial uploads and temporary artifacts cleaned up. |
| LT-9 | Approved: at most 16 open connections, at most 4 concurrently processed requests, finite connection, header and body read timeouts, explicit overload responses; tests for limits and resource cleanup. |
