# UD-32 Rank-based qualification

Status: **Confirmed by the user** (option Rank_Reference, with constraints)

Decision: **Rank_Reference**. One added expression operation, `rank_reference`, reads the Rank_Position of the participant under one declared ranking Target as an Exact_Number operand. A static cycle check returns `RANK_REFERENCE_CYCLE` (Severity Error). A Tie_Group is never cut at the cutoff: every member reads the shared Rank_Position and gets the same Outcome (Req 14.12 a to f).

Affects: Requirements 14.2, 14.5, 14.11 to 14.14, 31, 32.3, 32.4, 33.6. Design §8.9 (D-14), §8.5.

## User constraints (confirmed)

Two separate things, never merged:

1. **`rank_reference` (internal).** Reads the Rank_Position the engine computes from a ranking Rule declared in the Rulebook, for example group standings for top-two qualification. It is used only where an applicable competition rule explicitly declares it, and its provenance is that Rule's Source_Spans. An Indeterminate position gives an Indeterminate operand.
2. **External ranking values.** A ranking from an outside authority, such as a pre-event seeding list used as a late tie-breaker, is not `rank_reference`. It is an explicit input field in Competition_Data and must carry:
   - the ranking authority, the edition or effective date, and its provenance;
   - no fetched, inferred or invented values: the engine only reads what is supplied;
   - a missing or unverifiable value gives an Indeterminate Outcome (Req 11);
   - it is applied only at the exact position the competition's tie-breaking rules give it.
