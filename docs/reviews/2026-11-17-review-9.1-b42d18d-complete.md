## 4b. Non-blocking findings
- N-A The date property (`dates_are_accepted_exactly_when_a_reference_calendar_says_so`, 1024 cases, y in 0..=9999, m in 0..=14, d in 0..=33) does not detect leap-year mutants. Dropping the 100-year rule, dropping the 400-year rule and making February 29 invalid in leap years all survive the property and are killed only by the unit test `rt_app_0004_dates_are_valid_calendar_dates`. Cause: a leap day or a century year is hit with probability about 0.0005 per case. Suggest weighting the generator toward Feb 28/29/30 and years divisible by 4 and 100.
